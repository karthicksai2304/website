Education and clinical practice under one roof.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before contacting our center.

What courses do you offer?

We offer Diploma in Acupuncture and Master in Acupuncture programs.

Is practical training included?

Yes. Practical training, demonstrations and supervised clinical learning are included in the programs.

What is the duration of each course?

The stated duration for each course is 1 year. Please contact the center for the current batch schedule.

Who can join the courses?

Candidates who have completed 10th standard can apply, provided they can read, write and speak Tamil or English. Candidates from any degree background may also apply.

Is a certificate provided?

Yes. A certificate is provided on successful completion of the respective program.

Do you provide clinical training?

Yes. Clinical exposure and supervised practical training are part of the learning approach.

How can I book a clinic appointment?

You can contact the center by phone or WhatsApp to enquire about an appointment.

Where is the institute located?

How can I enquire about admission?

Use the enquiry form on this website or contact the institute directly by phone or WhatsApp.
